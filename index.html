The person behind the pixels

I'm a full-stack developer who treats the browser like a canvas. Over the last five years I've shipped products for startups and studios alike, always chasing the same goal: interfaces that feel alive without sacrificing an ounce of performance.

My focus lately is on interaction design — the small, tactile details that make a screen feel like it's responding to you specifically. Custom cursors, physics-based motion, 3D perspective tricks in plain CSS. No shortcuts, no heavy frameworks, just a deep respect for the fundamentals.
